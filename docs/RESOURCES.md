Private resources require authorized access and may appear unavailable to other visitors. A resource link does not establish public availability or release approval. GPU parity, complete reconstruction, data rights and the other conditions in [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.md) remain separately gated.

Dataset destinations `songdj/LevBench`, `songdj/Lev-train-v2` and `songdj/Lev-results` are planned and are not advertised as available downloads. See the [dataset cards](../data-cards/) and [data requirements](DATA.md). Source code, model artifacts, and dataset text have separate licensing and verification requirements.
